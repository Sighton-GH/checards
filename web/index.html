Starts a fresh game. Finished games stay saved in this browser.

Goal

Destroy both enemy Aces. If a long stretch passes with no combat, the game is a draw.

Your cards

Each side has 13 cards: a Joker, two Aces, and 9, 10, J, Q, K in each of its two suits. Red plays diamonds and hearts and always moves first; Black plays clubs and spades.

Setup

You place 7 cards, one at a time, on empty squares in your three home rows. Your two Aces go first, then you pick which card to place next. The 6 cards you don't place become your draw pile.

A turn

  • You get 3 moves per turn. One move shifts one of your cards to a neighbouring square, in any direction.
  • Up to 4 of your cards can share a square.
  • Or draw instead: as the first thing on your turn, place the top card of your draw pile on an empty square of your back row. That uses your whole turn.

Combat

Move onto a square that holds enemy cards to attack. Battles are fought at the end of the turn and every card involved is revealed. Each side's stack wins or dies as a whole.

  • An attacking Ace beats anything except a defending Joker. One attacking Ace trades with it; two attacking Aces destroy it and survive.
  • A defending Joker beats any attack that has no Ace.
  • Otherwise the higher total wins. Aces and Jokers count 1, cards 9 to K count their face value (K = 13). A tie destroys both stacks.
  • A card that attacks can't move again that turn.

Hidden cards

Enemy cards stay face down until they fight in a battle. Cards marked 👁️ have been seen by the other side - yours included, so you know what to keep hidden.

Playing here

  • Tap one of your cards, then tap a highlighted square to move it. If several cards on that square can make the move, you'll be asked which one.
  • When a draw is available, the empty squares on your back row glow. Tap one to draw there.
  • Your home rows are always at the bottom.
